Privacy policy

Duo Theme is provided by Jaehwan Kim. This policy describes the app, the support contact, and this website.

Data in the app

The app stores your theme names, messages, and selected symbols on your device in a shared app container so its widget can read them. This version does not include sign-in, advertising, analytics SDKs, or a feature that uploads your themes to a developer server. We do not sell your theme data or use it for targeted advertising.

Your choices and deletion

Delete saved themes from the My themes list. If you delete every theme, the built-in starter theme appears again. iOS manages app storage and any device backups according to your device settings. We do not operate a cloud account containing your themes. Exported images remain where you save or share them; delete those copies separately.

Sharing

When you choose to share an image, iOS sends it to the app or service you select. That service handles the image under its own policies. Do not include sensitive information in a theme you intend to display on your Lock Screen or share.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond to your request and retain correspondence while needed to resolve and follow up on it. You may request deletion by email, subject to any applicable legal retention obligation. Email providers process messages as part of delivery.

This static website does not add analytics or advertising scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under its privacy statement.

Children, changes, and contact

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. Contact us if a child has sent personal information to support. Updates to this policy will be published here. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email wosr1@naver.com.
