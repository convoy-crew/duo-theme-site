Personal Lock Screen themes

Make your Lock Screen feel like you.

Duo Theme lets you create a personal message and symbol, save themes, and choose one for a Lock Screen widget.

Get started

  1. Open Duo Theme and save your theme.
  2. Touch and hold the iOS Lock Screen, choose Customize, and tap the widget area.
  3. Add Duo Theme, then tap the widget to select your saved theme.
  4. To use a wallpaper, create an image in the app, save it from the share sheet, and apply it in iOS Wallpaper settings.

What to expect

You add widgets and apply wallpapers manually. iOS controls the widget layout and refresh timing. Always On Display requires a supported device and can turn off according to system settings and conditions. Duo Theme does not replace your entire Lock Screen or keep a screen permanently on.
